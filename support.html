Support

NestIQ private beta · v0

NestIQ is currently in private beta. We welcome questions, bug reports, and feature requests.

Email support

Response time: 24-48 hours during business days.

Reporting a bug

When you email us, please include:

— Which calculator or screen the issue is on
— What you did (steps to reproduce)
— What you expected to see
— What actually happened
— iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About)
— NestIQ version (Settings → bottom of screen)

Feature requests

We're actively developing. If there's a calculation, disclosure, or workflow you need for your origination practice, let us know — we're prioritizing based on beta feedback.

Regulatory questions

NestIQ output is not a regulatory disclosure. For compliance questions specific to your state licensing or institution, consult your compliance counsel. We're happy to discuss how the app presents disclosures and where its outputs fit into your workflow.
