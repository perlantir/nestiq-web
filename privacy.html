Privacy Policy

Effective April 19, 2026 · Private Beta v0

NestIQ ("the app") is a mortgage intelligence application developed by Uber Kiwi LLC, currently in private beta. This policy describes how NestIQ handles information.

What we collect

All scenario data, borrower information, and lender profile data you enter into NestIQ is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. NestIQ does not transmit this data to external servers, Uber Kiwi LLC, or any third party.

Authentication

NestIQ uses Sign in with Apple for user authentication. When you sign in, Apple may share your name and a unique identifier with the app. Apple's privacy practices apply to that flow. NestIQ does not receive your email address or Apple ID.

AI narration

NestIQ's narrative text generation feature ("Narrate") uses Apple's on-device FoundationModels framework (available on iOS 18.2 and later). All AI processing happens locally on your device. Your borrower data is not transmitted to any external AI service, OpenAI, Anthropic, or third party.

Photo library access

If you choose to add a profile photo to your lender profile, NestIQ accesses your photo library with your permission. The selected image is stored locally on your device.

Biometric authentication

If you enable Face ID to open the app, authentication happens entirely on-device using Apple's Face ID system. NestIQ does not receive or store biometric data.

Analytics

During private beta, NestIQ does not use third-party analytics, crash reporting services, or advertising identifiers.

Changes

This policy will be expanded before public App Store release to reflect any additional practices introduced.
